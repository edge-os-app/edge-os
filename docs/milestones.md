# Edge OS milestones

This is the six-month plan for an Orbio grant of USD 25,000. Each milestone ends in a test that anyone can check. All amounts are estimates, with no quotes. The order can change as the work teaches us more.

**Done so far:** a real study runs on [edgeos.fun](https://edgeos.fun) through Orbio. Three real calls matched the drop in the Orbio balance exactly. The study result was limited data. The site cannot connect a wallet or trade yet.

| Milestone | Months | Work | USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1, foundation | 1-2 | Signed permissions replace the limits in code. Agent and permission records. A core that is not tied to one chain, venue or provider. A new strategy record, with old records kept unchanged. | 3,500 |
| M2, complete research | 1-3 | CREDIT budgets on every call. Real research tools. X evidence. Recovery of failed jobs. More real history data. Statistics checks. Multi-position replay. | 3,700 |
| M3, live app and paying traders | 2-4 | Finish hosted checks. One research agent per user. Wallet creation and funding. Sign in with Orbio. Tests with real traders. | 4,000 |
| M4, live agents | 3-5 | Real in-app trades. An agent that runs its signed rules, with no model choosing a trade. Recovery after a failure. Sell, stop, withdraw, remove and renew permission. Recurring edge checks. | 5,500 |
| M5, fast entry and second chain | 4-5 | Paste a token address or link. An exit watcher for sell and stop terms. Token checks. A second venue. A first move to a second chain. | 3,000 |
| M6, LP traders | 5-6 | LP research, rules and simulation. Live LP trade support inside signed limits. | 1,800 |
| M7, perp and prediction market traders | 4-6 | Research and simulation on the same core. Live trades on existing venues such as Hyperliquid and Polymarket. Edge OS holds the signed limits, because venues do not. Records for liquidation and market resolution. | 3,500 |

## Done when

- **M1:** a signed permission replaces the limits in code, and Edge OS refuses a trade outside it. Old strategy records still load.
- **M2:** a real study's report shows the CREDIT cost of each call and the X evidence. A failed job recovers.
- **M3:** five real traders sign in with Orbio on the hosted app and finish a study paid from their own balance.
- **M4:** an agent makes a real in-app trade under its signed rules, then stops, sells and withdraws on command. The security review is finished.
- **M5:** a pasted token trades on a second venue, the watcher runs a sell or stop term, and one trade runs on a second chain.
- **M6:** a study tests an LP range on real history, and one small real LP position opens and closes inside signed limits.
- **M7:** one small real perp trade and one small real prediction market trade run inside signed limits. A test liquidation and a market resolution show correctly in the record.
