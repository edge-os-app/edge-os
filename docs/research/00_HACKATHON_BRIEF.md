## 9. Judging criteria

### Official judging rules

The authenticated catalog publishes a 100-point main-track rubric. The five weights are consistent across all four tracks. Each track applies a track-specific question under each heading. [S30]

| Official criterion | Weight | What it tests across the tracks |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical Execution | 20% | Correct end-to-end onchain behavior, not a static or simulated product surface |
| Design & Craft | 20% | Trustworthy user experience or developer experience appropriate to the track |
| Originality & Track Insight | 15% | A new track-relevant mechanism or experience, with a non-decorative onchain layer |
| Founder & Market Readiness | 25% | A specific first user, real problem knowledge, and credible reason for adoption |
| Traction & Path Forward | 20% | User or developer testing plus a concrete distribution, liquidity, launch, or integration plan |

Track-specific published checks: [S30]

| Track | Technical Execution, 20% | Design & Craft, 20% | Originality & Track Insight, 15% | Founder & Market Readiness, 25% | Traction & Path Forward, 20% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Onchain Finance & Trading | End-to-end onchain market mechanics with real settlement and pricing or matching | Clear prices, risks, and an interface trusted with capital | New primitive or structure enabled by fast settlement, not a faster clone | Specific trader, protocol, or counterparty and why the market is missing | Real tests plus a TVL, volume, liquidity, launch, or fundraising next step |
| Consumer Products & Payments | Real onchain conditional logic and settlement, not simulation | A non-crypto user completes the flow without help or crypto friction | New consumer finance experience, not a rebranded wallet | Specific consumer segment and demonstrated behavior or pain knowledge | User testing, even five people, plus a plan for the next 100 users |
| Social, Attention & Culture | Correct and verifiable onchain social, cultural, or governance mechanism | Inviting social behavior, not a token gate attached to a generic interface | Participation or attention creates verifiable stake, not decoration | Specific community or creator base and a reason it would migrate | Real community engagement plus a growth or creator-acquisition plan |
| Trust, Identity & AI Infrastructure | Correct and secure primitive, including sound WebAuthn, P256, key, and secret handling where used | Clear documentation and a clean interface or API for developers | Private, composable trust or ownership without recreating central capture | Specific adopting developers or apps and a reason to use the primitive | Developer interest, even one integration, plus a post-event adoption plan |

### Organizer or sponsor advice

The four `best fit` statements describe relevant experience. The authenticated rubric, not these statements, is the formal judging standard. [S1][S30]

0x praised KiSignal for a cohesive user flow, real utility, and seamless execution. It praised Gorillionaire for real-time signals, execution, and gamification. This is sponsor feedback from a prior event, not a Metropolis rule. [S14]

### Patterns inferred from previous winners

Several verified winners completed a visible user action instead of stopping at infrastructure. Several also made an onchain action part of the product loop. The supporting projects and limits are in section 12.

### Interpretation

The highest single weight is Founder & Market Readiness at 25%. Technical Execution, Design & Craft, and Traction & Path Forward each carry 20%. Originality carries 15%. This means technical work alone cannot earn more than one fifth of the published score. This is arithmetic from the official rubric, not an added criterion. [S30]

### Lessons for later idea selection

- Ask what user action ends in a verifiable Monad transaction.
- Ask whether Monad performs necessary work or only hosts a token or record.
- Ask what a judge can complete in under two minutes.
- Ask which proof can show the entire loop: interface state, transaction, explorer record, and changed result.
- Ask whether each sponsor integration has one necessary role.
- Ask whether the product can be scoped to a complete vertical slice in the remaining time.

These are later selection tests. They are not product candidates and do not choose a track.
