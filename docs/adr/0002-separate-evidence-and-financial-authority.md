# Separate evidence, operational eligibility and financial authority

The builder accepted independent claim-support, operational and permission gates on 2026-10-03, Q12 and Q15–Q17, with the single live flow and optional-trial amendment. A useful unresolved report can complete research. An unproven strategy can run only with qualified executable behavior and explicit bounded authority for its exact approved revision. Favorable research grants no spending permission. Consent does not improve evidence. This supports direct ongoing operation without compulsory backtesting, paper testing or graduation while preserving unfavorable findings.

The [readiness rules](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-edge-os-hackathon-design.md#accepted-claim-specific-support-and-readiness-gates) and [live flow](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-edge-os-hackathon-design.md#live-trading-optional-trials-and-forward-learning) own required behavior. Related rationale is in [ADR 0004, immutable revision binding](0004-immutable-strategy-revision-binding.md) and [ADR 0005, model/execution authority](0005-model-and-execution-authority-boundary.md). Exact publication/hash encoding and actual provider enforcement remain qualification/build-brief work. This records accepted requirements, not implemented safety or full-design approval.
