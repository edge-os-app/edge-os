# Bind evidence and permission to immutable strategy revisions

The builder accepted exact revision binding through the [readiness decisions](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-edge-os-hackathon-design.md#accepted-claim-specific-support-and-readiness-gates) and [live contract](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-edge-os-hackathon-design.md#live-trading-optional-trials-and-forward-learning), 2026-10-03. An immutable strategy revision preserves the reviewed meaning; changing that meaning requires a new revision and approval instead of silently inheriting evidence or authority through a mutable "latest" strategy.

This records the identity principle, not approval of the entire [publication protocol](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-edge-os-hackathon-design.md#revision-publication-and-draft-persistence). Canonical bytes/hash, manifest encoding, publication schemas and implementation checks remain build-brief/qualification work; no implementation or semantic-parity result is claimed.
