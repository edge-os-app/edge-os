# Account-view records

Version 2.0.0 adds P19-owned signed result and permission-history content. The reader accepts the original `account-view/1` rows and the new `account-view/2` rows. Each record uses the P02 envelope. P19 does not add its types to P02's closed schema registry.

Every record binds to the verified owner and exact agent. Wallet, network, asset, position, strategy revision, mandate, action, or flow identities are present when the record kind needs them. The server derives `owner_id` from authentication and rejects a client-supplied owner.

Holdings, balances, quantity, basis, capital flows and gas preserve `{asset_id, raw, decimals}` with a canonical non-negative base-10 `raw` value. Separate P19 `realized_result` and `estimated_unrealized_result` states use the same asset and decimal fields, but their canonical `raw` may be signed. `-0` and `+` are invalid. This represents the P15 quote-denominated result only; it does not convert assets, calculate percentages, or infer account value. Unknown stays `UNKNOWN` without an amount. A view does not sum amounts with different assets. Native MON gas stays separate. Deposits and withdrawals remain capital flows and do not count as profit or restore permission.

Each position's action list uses the exact owner, agent, wallet, network, and position identity. Wallet-level actions appear only under an exact flow and owner/agent/wallet/network identity in Funds. If the matching flow is not loaded, the action remains visible without flow attribution. An action from another wallet or position cannot appear under that flow or position. Closed positions, failed actions, and pending or unknown actions remain inspectable.

`PERMISSION_SNAPSHOT` records are separate immutable authored examples. They preserve the full owner/agent/wallet/network/strategy/mandate and approval/predecessor identities, reviewed terms and SHA256 over the canonical terms JSON, signer and policy bindings, recorded request/check/approval/application/failure/cancellation times, and explicit ACTIVE, ENDED, or NEVER_ACTIVE meaning. Lifecycle controls are state-specific: a pending change needs its recorded owner approval and predecessor but has no completed-check or application time; a failed attempt needs its completed check and failure time, with a predecessor only when it changes an existing approval; a cancelled attempt needs its cancellation time and may truthfully lack check, owner-approval, application, or predecessor fields. A missing predecessor identifies an initial attempt, not missing lineage. `CHANGE_APPLIED` marked `ACTIVE` means active at that recorded application time, not current authority. The current saved permission remains separately shown on the agent summary. The records are inspection-only; they cannot edit provider permission or activate an approval. `account-view/1` remains readable for prior records. `account-view/2` positions use signed result states, and permission snapshots require version 2.

This P19 fixture content uses only `SYNTHETIC_AUTHORED` evidence. The saved quantity on a CLOSED position is historical attribution to that saved position record, not residual current-wallet inventory. It cannot claim a live wallet, balance, health check, mark, receipt, account value, percent return, drawdown, or actual permission state. The P15 profile and record meanings remain controlling for later operational records.
