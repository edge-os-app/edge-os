# P12 spot replay profile — adopted contract

Version: `p12.spot-replay/1`. Contract status: builder-adopted on 2026-10-09. The recorded reply was: “okay I agree with your recommendations for p12, so you can proceed”. It adopts the three recommendations for proposal `a42942f5018ba902d7e597d902e19681012328d7`; the frozen adoption record is SHA256 `84d578170e32fcf4d1ac9861d772ae60797afa6fe9e19603321e6aa0e229b2c2`. This records model/contract decisions, not simulator implementation, a real dataset, executable fills, or financial authority. P12 does not qualify a market dataset or execution route.

## Authority and evidence status

- **Accepted:** P02 is locally integrated at `8706aa2679ed184adf479b5be426d7a48d7ddfde`. It expresses fixed positive quote sizing, one open position per strategy instance, `additions=false`, and `partial_exits=false`. P06 preserves five-minute UTC decisions, typed units, decimal-50 half-even arithmetic, whole-expression validation, scheduled history and reasoned UNKNOWN values. P07 defines research-history qualification and availability; no real pool history is qualified.
- **Adopted for P12/1:** the first replay model, supported subset, fill/mark timing, saved-cost formulas, ordering, accounting, metrics and `p12.essential-evidence/1` binding rules below. Implementation and qualification limits remain.
- **Synthetic:** The worked cases use invented strategy/data/configuration identities, prices, capital and costs. They check arithmetic only.
- **Not established:** P07 qualification is not fill/cost qualification. These files implement no simulator and make no profitability claim.

Every run must bind the immutable strategy revision, dataset revision, run-configuration identity, profile version, pool/quote identity, warm-up and half-open evaluation window. No identity may be silently substituted.

## Adopted first replay scope

Use one P06 strategy instance, one pool and one position; fixed positive quote size; full exits only; no additions or partial exits. The adopted P12 subset requires explicit P02 `invalidation: {state: NOT_REQUESTED, source_ref: ...}`. P02/P06 still accept and preserve Boolean invalidation rules; P12 defers those rules in this first release. This is an adopted P12 release boundary, not compiler behavior. A Boolean invalidation may not be rewritten to `NOT_REQUESTED`.

Reject unsupported input before replay with a specific unavailable/unsupported reason and retain the exact saved revision. This includes additions, partial exits, multiple positions, Boolean invalidation under the adopted P12/1 subset, missing cost provenance and cost inputs that produce a non-positive fill. The profile does not assign new behavior to invalidation. Supporting it would require a separately adopted meaning and ordering.

Keep partial-exit and competing-reservation arithmetic as separately scoped accounting conformance cases. They do not create runtime support. Do not add duplicate strategy slots or a portfolio model just to run a case. The adopted interpretation satisfies P12's independently calculated partial-exit check with the conformance example; competing-reservation accounting remains conditional on any future shared-capital multiple-position model. Production partial exits or portfolio support need a new authorized contract and scope.

The next-bucket close fill, cost formulas, valuation, essential-evidence binding and P12/1 narrowing are adopted contract terms. The synthetic 10 bps fee and zero slippage in the case file are not defaults.

## Adopted replay rules

1. **Admission and coverage.** Bind one immutable P06 strategy to one exact P07-qualified dataset revision, market identity, quote asset, profile/configuration, warm-up and UTC window `[start,end)`. Admission requires P07 qualification and proof that coverage is complete for the requested interval and dependencies; it does not require every observation to exist. A present `COMPLETE_EMPTY` bucket proves absence: quote volume is zero, but price has no close. `INCOMPLETE`, `UNQUALIFIED`, absent scheduled coverage, or unknown delivery means coverage is unproved and blocks the affected decision (or rejects admission if the bound interval cannot be proven). Keep per-decision gaps and their exact reason separate from dataset qualification. No real dataset is currently eligible.
2. **Calculation and action eligibility.** Invoke the saved P06 evaluator at each scheduled decision with the exact scheduled observations. Preserve its actual returned value and every reason. A determinate Kleene result such as `TRUE OR UNKNOWN` remains TRUE with its missing reason. A `price_return` with qualified endpoints retains its calculated return across an unused intermediate gap and also retains that gap reason. Validate the whole expression and every timing dependency; invalid trees and future-information dependencies cannot be hidden by a decisive sibling. UNKNOWN cannot trigger an action. The separate P12/1 eligibility gate checks hypothesis-essential evidence by the adopted versioned run binding below. Do not infer essentiality from a reason or final Boolean alone. Missing bound essential evidence makes the affected action-field decision ineligible with its exact evidence reason; a nonessential or operation-unused reason remains visible and does not by itself change a determinate result into UNKNOWN or suppress an action. An absent, incomplete or otherwise unresolved binding yields `UNRESOLVED_ESSENTIAL_EVIDENCE`; an identity mismatch yields `ESSENTIAL_BINDING_IDENTITY_MISMATCH`; malformed metadata yields `INVALID_ESSENTIAL_BINDING`. None is eligible for action. P02 has no P12 binding field; `p12.essential-evidence/1` is the adopted P12 run-configuration descriptor, not a P02/P06 change. An input is timely only if qualified and `P07.available_at <= decision_at`; bucket end alone does not prove delivery. Never shift, interpolate or carry an observation.

### Adopted essential-evidence binding descriptor

This is the adopted P12 run-configuration descriptor, not a P02 field or a change to P06. Its exact version is `p12.essential-evidence/1`. It binds one saved strategy revision, immutable run-configuration identity, dataset revision and canonical P02 market-binding identity. All four identity values must equal the values used by this run; a mismatch rejects the binding before any action as `ESSENTIAL_BINDING_IDENTITY_MISMATCH`. No identity can be inferred or substituted.

```yaml
schema_version: p12.essential-evidence/1
strategy_revision: <exact immutable strategy revision>
run_configuration_id: <exact immutable run configuration>
dataset_revision: <exact immutable qualified P07 dataset revision>
market_binding_id: <exact canonical P02 market-binding identity>
fields:
  entry:
    state: BOUND
    essential_dependencies:
      - series: price
        offset_buckets: 0
  exit:
    state: BOUND
    essential_dependencies: []
```

`fields` covers exactly `entry` and `exit`, separately. Each must be explicitly `BOUND` or `UNBOUND`; a missing field is not an empty set and is not eligible. A `BOUND` field must have an `essential_dependencies` list (possibly empty); `UNBOUND` carries no dependency list. Both fields must be `BOUND` before replay; an omitted field yields `UNRESOLVED_ESSENTIAL_EVIDENCE` and `ESSENTIAL_BINDING_FIELD_MISSING:<field>`, while explicit `UNBOUND` yields `UNRESOLVED_ESSENTIAL_EVIDENCE` and `ESSENTIAL_BINDING_FIELD_UNBOUND:<field>`, before an order. A `BOUND` field with `essential_dependencies: []` explicitly declares that its hypothesis has no additional essential observations. It does not mean P06 operation inputs are optional, all-reasons-ignored, ready, qualified or financially authorized. A binding object that is missing, incomplete or not associated with the exact run cannot make an action eligible. Boolean invalidation is outside this descriptor under the adopted P12/1 `NOT_REQUESTED`-only subset; do not assign it entry/exit meaning here.

Each dependency is the exact supported primitive series plus a scheduled-relative integer offset. The only supported series are `price` and `quote_volume`, and the series must be declared by the bound strategy and supplied by the bound dataset. Offset `0` means the current decision's scheduled bucket; `-1` means exactly the immediately prior scheduled bucket; `-n` means exactly `n` five-minute scheduled positions earlier. Positive offsets are invalid (`INVALID_ESSENTIAL_BINDING`); do not introduce a future lookup. Do not compress gaps, remap absolute observations, or change P06's declared warm-up/history. At decision position `i`, resolve `schedule[i + offset_buckets]`. A required position before the available schedule or absent from the bound dataset is unavailable, not a shifted lookback.

These checks are separate from P06 expression dependencies and full-tree validation. Evaluate the saved P06 expression normally and retain its exact value/reasons, including determinate `TRUE OR UNKNOWN` and a calculated endpoint return with an unused intermediate gap. Separately verify every bound essential observation against the exact dataset/market identity, schedule bucket, P07 qualification and explicit `available_at <= decision_time`. Missing `available_at` is not timely; bucket end alone never proves delivery. Missing/unqualified/late required evidence makes the affected field's decision ineligible as `ESSENTIAL_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`; preserve the original P07/source reason and every P06 reason (for example `FUTURE_INFORMATION:0`, `UNQUALIFIED_PRICE:0`, or `MISSING_PRICE:1`) in source order. An observation supplied for a different market or scheduled bucket retains P06's `MARKET_BINDING_MISMATCH:<position>` or `SCHEDULE_MISMATCH:<position>` and cannot satisfy the binding. Unknown fields/series, duplicate dependencies, wrong types, positive offsets or malformed objects are `INVALID_ESSENTIAL_BINDING`; they are not treated as empty. A syntactically valid binding for another strategy/run/dataset/market is the identity mismatch above. No invalid or unavailable binding falls back to assuming all reasons essential or none essential.

The descriptor and complete entry/exit preflight were adopted on 2026-10-09. The binding values must still be authored from the actual user's hypothesis; P12 does not choose an essential set on the user's behalf. Even a valid empty set leaves mandatory strategy/dataset admission, P06 whole-tree/operation/timing checks, P07 qualification/availability, fill/mark/cost, ordering, sizing and supported-subset rules in force. It grants no general readiness or financial permission. WC-06 and WC-14 give synthetic literal examples, including a lookback resolved anew at two decision positions.
3. **Time, fills and terminal sample.** At a timestamp shared with an earlier order, settle that order first only if its required close is qualified and available by `fill_at`; next value the account from that same bucket close if it is qualified and available; then evaluate the current signal using that same immutable pool/bucket close. Do not supply two different `price()` values for one market/bucket. A signal at `t` cannot fill at its signal-bucket close. The adopted fill proxy is the close of the next scheduled five-minute bucket, available by that bucket's end under the dataset's explicit P07 availability model. It is not executable liquidity, latency, capacity or Kuru fill evidence. A late, missing, empty, incomplete or unqualified close means no fill; record its reason, release that order's reservation at the scheduled fill time and do not retry the old signal. If the next bucket end is outside `[start,end)`, record `NO_NEXT_BUCKET` at `t`; no reservation carries beyond the run. The exclusive `end` timestamp is not a decision, fill or mark. The adopted terminal sample is the last scheduled bucket end `t` satisfying `start <= t < end`. At that `t`, settle any prior fill, then value the account from that bucket's own qualified/available close, then evaluate the terminal decision; an order with no next in-window close receives `NO_NEXT_BUCKET` and cannot reserve. If the terminal mark is unavailable, preserve known cash/inventory and make terminal value and dependent gain/loss/drawdown unavailable with the exact reason. Do not substitute the exclusive-end close or carry an earlier mark.
4. **Order and reservation sequence.** Settle the previous order and consume/release its reservation; value the account; evaluate current conditions. While open, evaluate an eligible full exit first. If true, reserve all attributable base and create no entry on that decision. If exit is false, no exit is created; if it is unknown/ineligible, preserve value/reasons and do not infer false. When flat, an exactly TRUE eligible entry may reserve fixed quote notional plus fee. Reservations do not change account value before fill; never reserve cash or base twice. Reject a full request that does not fit as `INSUFFICIENT_CASH`; do not resize. One instance has no production competing-position tie-break. WC-02 is a separate hypothetical conformance case only.
5. **Supported subset.** The P02-supported shape has fixed positive quote size, one open position per instance, full exits, `additions=false`, and `partial_exits=false`. Adopted P12/1 further requires explicit `NOT_REQUESTED` invalidation; return `UNSUPPORTED_INVALIDATION` before replay for a valid Boolean rule. P02/P06 continue to validate it and preserve its source identity. Do not infer if Boolean invalidation means entry gate, full exit or both. Every rejection names the original field and reason. Partial-exit calculations are not runtime support. This adopted P12 release boundary does not change or shrink P02/P06 contracts.
6. **Saved cost assumptions.** P02/P06 store canonical decimal `fee_rate` and `slippage_rate` fractions in `[0,1]`, plus `source_ref`. Adopted P12/1 uses these exact saved rates as authoritative and retains the exact values and source reference in every run. Do not silently substitute zero, alter the immutable strategy or override either rate. Basis-point display is `rate × 10,000` only. For a buy, apply adverse slippage to the next-bucket close first: `buy_fill = close × (1 + slippage_rate)`; acquired base is `quote_notional / buy_fill`. For a sell, `sell_fill = close × (1 - slippage_rate)`; calculate gross quote and fee at that price. Fee is charged per side: buy fee is `quote_notional × fee_rate`; sell fee is `gross_quote × fee_rate`. A derived non-positive fill is unsupported before action. Scenario overrides are not supported in this version. A future override must be an explicit immutable run setting that records original rates/source and override rates/source, names precedence, and is separately adopted. Gas, separate spread, routing, impact beyond this declared slippage proxy and capacity are excluded; output is not all-in net performance. Missing/unusable cost source or value never becomes zero.
7. **Accounting and marks.** Denominate in the declared quote asset. Prices are quote/base; sizing, cash and fees are quote; holdings are base. Debit buy notional plus fee; credit sell gross less fee. Include entry fee once in weighted-average basis and allocate basis proportionally in accounting examples; subtract each exit fee once. Account value is `cash_quote + open_base × qualified_mark_quote_per_base`. The mark is that scheduled bucket's own close at its P07 availability time, not a slippage-adjusted fill. No earlier mark carries forward. A missing/late mark preserves known cash and inventory but makes that account value and dependent metrics unavailable with its reason.
8. **Metrics.** There are no deposits or withdrawals. Initial capital is the starting account value. Gain/loss is final account value minus initial capital, in quote units. Trade count is completed entry/exit pairs. Largest drawdown is the maximum peak-to-later-trough decline in sampled account values, an absolute quote amount. If a sample needed for the full interval is unavailable, the full-period metric is unavailable. Percentage return and annualization are outside this profile.
9. **Records and repeatability.** Preserve open end inventory with base quantity, weighted basis, last qualified mark and its time/availability. Do not call an unknown mark zero or realized profit. Chronologically record original strategy/dataset/configuration/profile identities; each observation's status, availability and reason; P06 calculated value/reasons; separate eligibility; decision; order; reservation; fill/rejection; fee; cash; holdings; mark; and metric status. Replaying the same immutable input identities and calculation version must produce identical ordered results. WC-10 checks a fully stated literal trace only; it does not prove runtime persistence or idempotency.

## Adopted decisions and consequences

| Choice | Adopted P12/1 term | Alternative not selected / consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Fill timing | Next scheduled bucket close, only once available | Same-bucket close risks future information; event-level fills need another data model. |
| Dataset | Exact P07-qualified immutable dataset and `[start,end)` per run | No real pool is qualified; synthetic evidence is not real coverage. |
| Cost precedence | Use the saved P02 fractional fee and slippage with stated formulas and `source_ref`; no implicit overrides | A run override needs its own immutable config, retained original values/source and adoption. Ignoring nonzero saved slippage changes the strategy's stated cost assumptions. |
| Essential-evidence eligibility | Preserve P06 values/reasons; `p12.essential-evidence/1` binds dependencies separately for entry and exit, with complete-pair preflight | P02 has no P12 binding field. Treating every reason as essential over-blocks accepted determinate/unused-gap results, while treating none as essential can ignore the hypothesis. Actual sets remain user-supplied. |
| Invalidation | Require explicit `NOT_REQUESTED` for P12/1 | Boolean invalidation's action meaning/order remains undefined and is deferred; this is a P12-supported-subset boundary, not a P02/P06 restriction. |
| Mark/metrics | Own-bucket qualified close at the last scheduled in-window sample; quote value, gain/loss and absolute drawdown | Stale marks hide gaps; percentages/annualization need extra choices. |
| Partial exits/shared reservations | Keep independently calculated conformance cases outside one-position runtime | Runtime support requires explicit scope and, for multiple positions, a portfolio/reservation model. |

These choices were adopted on 2026-10-09 for `p12.spot-replay/1` and `p12.essential-evidence/1`. Alternatives were not selected. Any future change requires a versioned profile update and fresh independent calculations/reviews. Adoption does not qualify data, fills, costs or a runtime.

## Acceptance mapping

| Requirement | Adopted contract disposition | Remaining implementation/qualification limit |
|---|---|---|
| Exact dataset, coverage and version | Exact P07 revision, bindings, warm-up and half-open window | Real data remains unqualified; qualify a real dataset before any real replay |

| Coincident order/cash behavior | Prior order settles before current signal; full exit suppresses same-decision entry; reservations explicit | Implement and verify adopted ordering/fill terms; WC cases are independent literals, not runtime verification |
